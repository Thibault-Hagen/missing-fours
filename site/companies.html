Companies

Every company with at least one quarter tested, ranked by how unlikely its count of 4s would be if the digit were random.

4s expected

Chance by luck is the probability of seeing this few 4s (or fewer) if each quarter's digit were random. The false-alarm rate (a Benjamini–Hochberg q-value) adjusts that for testing thousands of companies at once: it estimates the share of companies flagged at this level that would be flagged by luck alone. Companies with fewer than 20 tested quarters have no probability.

Built by Thibault Hagen. Data from SEC EDGAR XBRL filings, rebuilt every week.

A shortage of 4s is a statistical pattern. On its own it does not show that any company broke a rule. Nothing here is investment advice.

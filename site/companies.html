The Missing 4s
Story Companies Method

Companies

Every company with at least one quarter tested, ranked by how far its 4s and 5s are from what a random digit would produce.

Each company with 20 or more tested quarters gets two tests. "Chance of so few 4s" is the probability of seeing that many 4s or fewer if each quarter's digit were random; "chance of so many 5s" is the same for 5s, from the other side. The false-alarm rate (a Benjamini–Hochberg q-value) adjusts the stronger of the two for running both tests on every company: it estimates the share of companies flagged at that level that would be flagged by luck alone. Companies with fewer than 20 tested quarters have no probability.

Built by Thibault Hagen. Data from SEC EDGAR XBRL filings, rebuilt every week.

A shortage of 4s is a statistical pattern. On its own it does not show that any company broke a rule. Nothing here is investment advice.
